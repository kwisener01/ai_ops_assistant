## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This SOP defines the standard procedures for transmission assembly operations, ensuring consistent quality, safety, and efficiency in manufacturing processes.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all production and assembly personnel, supervisors, and team leads involved in transmission manufacturing operations.

## 3. Daily Production Procedures

### 3.1 Pre-Shift Activities (30 minutes before start)

**Step 1: Equipment Inspection**
- Verify all equipment is operational
- Check for any maintenance tags or warnings
- Test emergency stop buttons
- Confirm tool availability and condition

**Step 2: Material Verification**
- Confirm parts availability for scheduled production
- Verify part numbers match work orders
- Check material quality (no damage, correct specifications)
- Report any shortages to Material Handler

**Step 3: Safety Check**
- Put on required PPE (safety glasses, steel-toed boots, gloves)
- Verify safety guards are in place
- Check lighting and ventilation
- Review any safety alerts or bulletins

**Step 4: Shift Handover**
- Review handover notes from previous shift
- Understand any ongoing issues or concerns
- Confirm production targets for the shift
- Clarify any special instructions

### 3.2 Production Execution

**Step 1: Work Order Review**
- Read and understand work order requirements
- Verify part numbers and quantities
- Check for any special customer requirements
- Note quality checkpoints

**Step 2: Assembly Operations**
- Follow documented work instructions
- Use torque specifications as defined
- Apply proper sequence of assembly
- Use correct tools and equipment
- Mark completed steps on checklist

**Step 3: In-Process Quality Checks**
- Perform self-inspection at each critical stage
- Verify dimensions and tolerances
- Check for proper fit and alignment
- Document measurements as required
- Place quality checkmark on routing sheet

**Step 4: Issue Reporting**
- Immediately report any quality issues
- Document issue in AI Ops Assistant system
- Tag defective parts or assemblies
- Notify Team Lead and Quality Control
- Do not proceed if safety risk exists

**Step 5: Documentation**
- Record production counts every hour
- Log any downtime with reason codes
- Complete routing sheets accurately
- Sign off on completed work orders

### 3.3 End-of-Shift Activities (30 minutes before end)

**Step 1: Production Summary**
- Count completed units
- Calculate production efficiency
- Document any incomplete work orders
- Prepare handover notes

**Step 2: Workspace Cleanup (5S)**
- **Sort**: Remove unnecessary items
- **Set in Order**: Organize tools and materials
- **Shine**: Clean workstation and equipment
- **Standardize**: Follow cleaning checklist
- **Sustain**: Prepare for next shift

**Step 3: Equipment Shutdown**
- Power down equipment per shutdown procedure
- Secure all tools in designated locations
- Lock out/tag out if maintenance required
- Report any equipment issues

**Step 4: Shift Handover Meeting**
- Brief incoming shift on status
- Highlight any issues or concerns
- Transfer custody of incomplete work
- Update shift log

## 4. Production Planning and Scheduling

### 4.1 Weekly Planning Process

**Monday Morning Planning Meeting (8:00 AM - 9:00 AM)**
- Review production forecast for the week
- Allocate resources to work orders
- Identify potential bottlenecks
- Coordinate with Maintenance for scheduled downtime
- Communicate plan to all shifts

### 4.2 Daily Production Meetings

**Daily Stand-up (15 minutes at shift start)**
- Yesterday's production results
- Today's production targets
- Known issues or challenges
- Safety reminders
- Questions and clarifications

## 5. Quality Standards

### 5.1 First-Pass Yield Target
- Minimum 98% first-pass yield
- Track defects by type and workstation
- Root cause analysis for recurring issues

### 5.2 Inspection Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Frequency | Responsibility | Documentation |
|------------|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| Incoming parts | Every batch | Material Handler | Receiving log |
| Sub-assembly | Each unit | Operator | Routing sheet |
| Final assembly | Each unit | Operator | Routing sheet |
| Pre-packaging | Sample (1:10) | QC Inspector | Inspection report |

### 5.3 Non-Conforming Product
- Immediately segregate defective items
- Apply red tag with issue description
- Log issue in AI Ops Assistant
- Notify Quality Department
- Do not proceed with defective components

---

## 6. Safety Procedures

### 6.1 Required PPE
- Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1 certified)
- Steel-toed boots
- Hearing protection in designated areas
- Cut-resistant gloves for sharp components
- Long pants and sleeves (no loose clothing)

### 6.2 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)
- Required for any maintenance or adjustment
- Only authorized personnel may perform LOTO
- Follow 6-step LOTO procedure
- Verify zero energy state before work begins

### 6.3 Emergency Procedures
- Know location of emergency exits
- Understand evacuation routes
- Emergency stop buttons marked and accessible
- First aid kits and eye wash stations identified
- Report all injuries immediately, no matter how minor

---

## 7. Downtime Management

### 7.1 Downtime Categories

| Category | Definition | Response |
|----------|------------|----------|
| **Planned** | Scheduled breaks, meetings, maintenance | No action needed |
| **Material Shortage** | Waiting for parts | Notify Materials, log in system |
| **Equipment Failure** | Machine breakdown | Call Maintenance, log issue |
| **Quality Hold** | Investigation or rework | Notify QC, log issue |
| **Changeover** | Product switching | Follow changeover procedure |

### 7.2 Downtime Reporting
- Log all downtime >5 minutes
- Use standardized reason codes
- Enter into AI Ops Assistant
- Include start/end times
- Supervisor approval required

## 8. Continuous Improvement

### 8.1 Idea Submission
- All employees encouraged to submit improvement ideas
- Use Continuous Improvement form
- Ideas reviewed weekly by management
- Recognition for implemented ideas

### 8.2 Problem-Solving Process
1. **Identify**: Clearly define the problem
2. **Contain**: Implement temporary fix if needed
3. **Analyze**: Use 5-Whys and fishbone diagram
4. **Solve**: Implement corrective action
5. **Verify**: Confirm effectiveness
6. **Standardize**: Update procedures

## 12. Escalation Path

| Issue Type | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Quality Issue | Team Lead | Shift Supervisor | Production Manager |
| Safety Concern | Team Lead (immediate) | Safety Manager | Plant Manager |
| Equipment Failure | Team Lead | Maintenance Manager | Production Manager |
| Material Shortage | Team Lead | Materials Manager | Operations Manager |

**Escalation Timeframes:**
- Critical (safety/production stop): Immediate
- High (quality/significant delay): Within 1 hour
- Medium (process issue): Within 4 hours
- Low (minor concern): Next shift meeting
